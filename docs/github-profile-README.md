## 怎么合作

1. 先聊一次（不收费）：把你最头疼的一件事讲清楚。
2. 我和你一起定一条路：先验证哪个假设、先做什么、后做什么、大概多久。
3. 小步做、快速验：按里程碑交付，用真实业务结果验收；不对就停，值得做再加码。

不适合：只要按页面照抄、不需要业务方参与的纯外包。

I help companies with complex businesses and heavy legacy systems turn their systems, processes, and AI into things people actually use.

## I don't just build software

I work inside your business, apply lean methodology, and keep validating the commercial assumptions with you: small steps, fast experiments, and no budget burned on features nobody uses.

## Who I help

| You are | Stuck on | What I do |
|---|---|---|
| Owner of a traditional business | A system nobody dares touch; docs gone; one change breaks three things | Rebuild the business model first, then refactor without stopping operations |
| Company lead | Software is bought, but the frontline still runs on Excel and chat; numbers don't reconcile | Connect process, data, and roles so work actually happens in the system |
| Leader exploring AI | No idea where AI pays off, or whether it does | Pick scenarios by role and process; ship tools used daily, not a demo |
| Founder / product lead | An idea, but no clear first step or delivery path | Positioning, first version, and a system that survives real users; validate in small steps |
| Transaction / inventory / hardware | Crashes under load; money and orders must be correct; hardware and software must ship together | High-concurrency, high-availability, consistency; device-to-cloud-to-back-office delivery |

We've rebuilt a 10+ year-old legacy system that was still running, and delivered 0-to-1 digitalization and AI transformation for a traditional company, a financial trading system, and an IoT project combining hardware and software. When a project needs more hands, my partners and I staff it as a team, not a last-minute bench.

## How we work

1. A first conversation, free: describe the one problem that hurts most.
2. We define a path together: which assumption to test first, what to do first, what next, and roughly how long.
3. Build in small steps and validate: milestone-based delivery against real business results; stop if it doesn't work, invest more only when it does.

Not a fit: pure outsourcing that copies pages and doesn't involve the business side.
